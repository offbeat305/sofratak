# Sofratak Brand Kit

Slogan EN: "Take Control. Own Your Growth." · AR: "شغلك تحت سيطرتك"
Primary CTA: "Calculate Your Savings" · Secondary: "Book a Demo"
Feel: premium hospitality + modern SaaS + trusted operator. Never: generic SaaS template, cheap food app, marketing-agency look, gold overload, stereotype Middle Eastern theming.

## Colors (make these Tailwind tokens)
- olive #2F4A3C — primary: nav, sidebar, hero backgrounds, headings, icons, selected states
- sand #D8C19A — secondary: soft card accents, badges (use lightly, no big beige sections)
- brass #A9792B — accent: primary CTA buttons, savings/money numbers, key stats, active states. Means money/growth. ~8% of any screen max.
- ivory #F7F2E8 — main background (prefer over pure white)
- charcoal #1F1F1F body text · stone #6B6B6B secondary text · clay #A56B52 warnings/rare warm accent (~2% max)
- Visual ratio: 45% ivory/neutral, 25% olive, 15% sand, 8% brass, 5% charcoal/stone, 2% clay. Calm and premium.
- Real red for errors, restrained green (distinct from olive) for positive deltas. Usability beats brand color.

## Typography
- Cormorant Garamond (SemiBold/Bold): marketing hero + major section headlines ONLY. Never inside dashboard UI.
- Manrope (400/500/600/700): all UI, body, buttons, tables, dashboards. Metrics/revenue = Manrope Bold.
- Arabic UI: IBM Plex Sans Arabic (clean, never ornamental).

## Components
- Cards: 20–28px radius, ivory/white, subtle border + shadow, generous padding
- Buttons: primary = brass bg + olive text · secondary = olive border/text on transparent or ivory · dark = olive bg + ivory text. 12–18px radius or pill.
- Inputs 12–16px radius, modals 24px
- Icons: clean geometric outline (lucide). BANNED: chef hats, forks, shawarma art, lanterns, crescents, mosques, heavy calligraphy. Arabic influence = warmth + subtle arch shapes + refined geometric dividers only.
- Motion: subtle fades, slight rise on scroll, card hover lift, number count-up. Nothing bouncy/flashy.

## Voice
Short, confident, practical. Talk money, time, orders, control. Banned: ecosystem, synergy, revolutionary, disruptive, omnichannel, digital transformation. Never "guaranteed savings" — always "Estimated/Potential Savings" + disclaimer: "Illustrative estimate based on the information provided. Actual results may vary."

## Design check before any screen is "done"
Premium? Restaurant-specific? Communicates control + financial value? Simple? Trustworthy? Works in Arabic RTL? Avoids cliché Middle East design? On-palette? Consistent with the rest of the product?
